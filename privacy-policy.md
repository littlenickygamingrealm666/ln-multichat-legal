# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 2025**

Little Nicky Multi Chat ("the App") is a free, open-source desktop overlay tool 
for live streamers. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

## Data We Collect
The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any external 
server. There is no backend server, database, or analytics system of any kind.

## Authentication Tokens
When you log in with TikTok or Twitch, your OAuth tokens are stored **locally 
on your own device** in a JSON file. These tokens are never sent to the App 
developer or any third party. They are used solely to connect to your live 
stream on your behalf.

## TikTok Data Usage
The App uses the TikTok Login Kit and requests the `user.info.basic` scope to 
retrieve your username in order to connect to your TikTok Live stream. 
Real-time chat messages, gifts, follows, and shares from your live stream are 
displayed as an on-screen overlay only. This data is processed locally on your 
device and is never stored or shared.

## Third-Party Services
The App connects to TikTok and Twitch APIs on your behalf. Your use of those 
platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies.

## Contact
If you have any questions, please open an issue on the GitHub repository.
